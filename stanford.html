Stanford

  • Die Luft der Freiheit weht.
  • Get a zipcar and take all your friends to Safeway at 3 AM for a late night snack run. (Legends are born at the Secret Safeway, but I won't tell you which one that is.)
  • Ask the folks at Late Nite to make you a milkshake with "everything." They should know what that means.
  • Alternatives to fountain hopping: Climb the McMurtry rooftop, eat an Ike's sandwich, go to Maus after dark, bring a floatie to Lake Lag, climb the gutters behind MemChu, invite yourself on a tour of the steam tunnels...
  • I am told that they renovated and fortified the Main Quad crawlspace but I'm sure that alone won't stop Stanford's best.
